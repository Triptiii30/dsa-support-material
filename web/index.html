💡 Technical Challenges & Corrective Actions

1. Duplicate Keys in AVL Tree: Overwriting prevented by implementing bucket lists in each node.

2. Negative Subsidies in Routing: Dijkstra fails on negative edge credits. Solved by routing subsidized freight through Bellman-Ford.

3. Disconnected Transportation Islands: Graph connected components identify unreachable clusters prior to dispatch.
