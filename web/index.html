💡 Technical Challenges & Corrective Actions

1. Duplicate Keys in AVL & B+ Trees: Overwriting prevented by attaching bucket lists to each node rather than duplicate nodes.

2. Negative Subsidies in Routing: Dijkstra fails on negative edge credits. Solved by routing subsidized freight through Bellman-Ford.

3. Combinatorial Explosion in Routing: Solved by Least-Cost Branch and Bound with Matrix Reduction, pruning 15 branches on 5 hubs.
